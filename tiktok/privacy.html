Privacy Policy — 100xAgent Publisher

Last updated: 23 September 2026

The app only uses the TikTok permissions user.info.basic (to show which account is connected) and video.upload (to send video drafts to the account owner's inbox).

We store only the OAuth tokens needed to upload drafts, encrypted in GitHub Actions secrets. We do not collect, sell or share any personal data, followers, messages or analytics.

To delete data, revoke the app's access in TikTok settings; tokens then stop working. Contact: via TikTok @shophaoquang25589.
