Privacy Policy — 100xAgent Publisher

Last updated: 8 October 2026

The app uses the TikTok permissions user.info.basic (to show which account is connected), video.upload (to send video drafts to the account's inbox) and video.publish (to publish video content directly to the account, honoring the creator's chosen privacy level and content disclosure settings).

We store only the OAuth tokens needed to upload/publish content, encrypted in GitHub Actions secrets. We do not collect, sell or share any personal data, followers, messages or analytics.

To delete data, revoke the app's access in TikTok settings; tokens then stop working. Contact: via TikTok @shophaoquang25589.
